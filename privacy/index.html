Hisaab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Hisaab is a money app made by Shikhar Kansal in India. This policy explains what the Hisaab app on Google Play reads, what it keeps, and where it keeps it. Questions: shikharkansal.sk@gmail.com.

The short version
  • Everything Hisaab keeps stays on your phone.
  • There is no account, no Hisaab server, no ads and no analytics.
  • The Google Play version has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What Hisaab reads, and why

Hisaab only reads these things if you allow them. You can turn any of them off in your phone's Settings.

PermissionWhat it readsWhy
Notification access Payment notifications from bank and UPI apps on your phone. Notifications from other apps are ignored. To record a payment's amount, payee and time, so Hisaab can ask what it was for.
Usage access Which apps were used on your phone, and when. Used on your phone only, to help Hisaab notice payments it should ask you about.
Contacts (optional) The saved name for a phone number, only when you turn this on from the You tab. When a payment shows only a phone number, Hisaab can show the person's name instead.
Show notifications Nothing. This lets Hisaab post its own notifications. To ask "What was that for?" after a payment.
Run after restart Nothing. This lets Hisaab start after your phone restarts. To bring back questions you had not answered yet.

Hisaab on Google Play does not read your SMS messages and does not use your microphone.

What Hisaab keeps

Hisaab stores the payments it records, the reasons you type, the sets (categories) you choose, and the budget numbers you enter, such as your monthly money, bills and planned buys. All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you move it yourself. Hisaab has no server, no account and no cloud sync. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting tools, and it does not send your data to any AI service.

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Hisaab. You can save a backup file yourself from the You tab, and you choose where it goes. If you save it to a cloud drive or send it to someone, that service's own privacy policy applies to that file.

If Hisaab offers a paid unlock, Google Play handles the payment. Hisaab never sees your card, bank or UPI details.

Sharing and selling

Hisaab does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Open the You tab and choose "Delete everything on this phone". Uninstalling the app also removes all Hisaab data from your phone. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

Your choices

You decide which permissions to grant, and you can withdraw them at any time in Android Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
