How it works

Three simple ideas. No spreadsheets, no bank login.

1

One number for today

Money left this month, divided by the days left, after your bills and planned buys. Spend more one day and tomorrow's number adjusts. Go past it and Hisaab says "Over by", not a scary minus.

2

"What was that for?"

After a UPI payment, Hisaab asks what it was for. You type the answer in your own words. No preset buttons, so your history reads like you wrote it.

3

Sets that remember

Each payment sits in a set like food or travel, remembered for that payee. Money you move, like savings, EMIs, card bills or transfers between your own accounts, never counts as spent.

Your money stays on your phone.

Hisaab is built so there is nothing to leak. The Google Play version does not even ask for internet access.
