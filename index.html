One question after every UPI payment

When you pay, Hisaab asks what it was for. You type the answer in your own words. There are no preset buttons, so your list of payments reads the way you think about your money.

  • Each payment goes into a set, like food or travel, and Hisaab remembers it for that payee.
  • Savings, EMIs, card bills and transfers between your own accounts are not counted as spending.
