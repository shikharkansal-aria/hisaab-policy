Hisaab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Hisaab is made by Shikhar Kansal. If you have questions, contact shikharkansal.sk@gmail.com .

What Hisaab reads

Hisaab reads payment-related notifications and app-usage events on your Android phone. These may include bank payment notifications and payment alerts from UPI apps.

Why Hisaab reads it

Hisaab uses this information to notice UPI payment activity and ask what a payment was for. It also shows your recorded payments in the app.

Where it is stored

Your Hisaab data is stored on your phone. This version does not require an account and does not send this data to a Hisaab server.

What is shared

Hisaab does not sell or share your payment data with other companies. This version has no advertising.

How to delete your data

You can delete your Hisaab data using the delete option inside the app.

Your rights

You can choose whether to grant Hisaab access to notifications and app-usage information. You can withdraw permissions in Android Settings and delete your data in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, email shikharkansal.sk@gmail.com .

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
